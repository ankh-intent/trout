Ranking by priority

Method: a weighted-sum model. Each trait is rescaled to 0–100 across every listed model (100 = best in this set, 0 = worst), then combined with the three weights set by the knob.

Priority

Drag the knob or use the arrow keys. A corner gives that trait 100%; the centre splits evenly.

Provider profiles

One shape per provider: the three scores of its best model for the current priority (its flagship when ranking flagships only). 0 at the centre, 100 at a corner. The dotted triangle is the current priority. Hover a provider to bring it forward; click it to hide or show it.
Price contribution Intelligence contribution Speed contribution
score = w(price) × price + w(intelligence) × intelligence + w(speed) × speed, weights sum to 100%

Price scores higher when lower; intelligence and speed score higher when higher. Intelligence is rescaled linearly; price and speed on a log scale, because they span orders of magnitude. "Avg rank" is the row's mean position over every point of the triangle for the chosen price measure: one figure that does not depend on where the knob sits. When ranking each provider's best model, the model in a provider's row changes with the knob, and its average rank follows the provider. Scores are relative to the listed models only, so adding or removing one shifts the rest. Models missing the chosen price measure are listed unranked; a missing speed is filled with the group median and marked *.
